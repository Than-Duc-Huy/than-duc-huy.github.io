## Github

This is a design project for the Body-Centric Design course at the School of Art Design Media (ADM) in NTU.
Check out the project's [Github Repository](https://github.com/Than-Duc-Huy/Pegas-US)

## Project Description

Our product includes an expressive lower leg prosthetics and an arm piece wearable to foster connection, involving the amputee's loved ones in their emotional journey throughout the recovery, rehabilitation process.

### Fabrication

The project includes 2 pieces

| 3D Printed Leg Piece                                                                                                                   | Textile Arm Wearable                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The leg piece is a reimagined design<br> of the prosthetics, allowing <br>self-expression beyond the normal<br> functional prosthetics | The arm piece is a wearable for the loved ones,<br> allowing them to experience the attention <br>that the amputee experiences.<br> However, the attention is not of malice <br> but of curiosity and awe |
| The movement will control the **red** light                                                                                            | The movement will control the **blue** light                                                                                                                                                              |
| <img src="/assets/img_post/bcd/leg.jpg" width="200">                                                                                   | <img src="/assets/img_post/bcd/arm.jpg" width="250">                                                                                                                                                      |

- The lighting and the speed is reflected in both pieces
- When the 2 lights overlap, magenta light will be shown

### Electronics

- Each piece has an LED strip that is controlled by an Arduino Nano and the communication between the two is done via Bluetooth with ESP32. The measurement of the movement is done via MPU6050, measuring the acceleration and angular acceleration.
