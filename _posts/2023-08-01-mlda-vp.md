In 2023, I was voted to be the Vice President of Academics in the Machine Learning and Data Analytics (MLDA@EEE)

## Workshop Hosting

<img src="/assets/img_post/2023-08-01-mlda-vp/google.jpg">

One of the main duty of Academics Portfolio is to organize and host workshops to teach and share knowledge with the EEE student body in various domains that our subcommittee are capable of: machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, robotics.

### MLDA Workshop series

We have our MLDA weekly workshops, organized in a series lasting a year to teach our student the fundamental of machine learning and deep learning.
