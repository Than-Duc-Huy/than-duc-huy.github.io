In 2023, I was voted to be the Vice President of Academics in the Machine Learning and Data Analytics (MLDA@EEE)

## Workshops

<img src="/assets/img_post/mlda/google.jpg">

The main duty of Academics Portfolio is to organize workshops to teach and share the knowledge with the EEE student body in various domains that our subcommittee member are capable of: machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, robotics.

We have our MLDA weekly workshops, organized in a series lasting a year to teach our student the fundamental of machine learning and deep learning.

## GPU Server Management

MLDA has GPU servers and workstations, managed by the students for the student projects. I was in charge of managing both the newer 3090 remote GPU server for the project teams as well as the 2080Ti on-site GPU workstations for learning and teaching purposes.
