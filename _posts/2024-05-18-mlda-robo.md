During my final year in [Machine Learning and Data Analytics Club (MLDA@EEE)](/posts/mlda), I led the Robotics Team initiative, gathering a group of EEE friends who are interested in Robotics participate in the BARN Challenge (Benchmark Autonomous Robot Navigation) at ICRA 2024.

Compared to [Garage@EEE](/posts/garage), MLDA@EEE is a software-focused club. Hence, our robotics projects require commercially available robot hardware or simulated environment.

## [BARN Challenge at ICRA 2024](https://cs.gmu.edu/~xiao/Research/BARN_Challenge/BARN_Challenge24.html#Simulation)

### Simulation

The organisers provided the simulation setup of 300 pre-generated world of varying difficulties for the benchmarking of the algorithm. We utilized the the global planner from ROS1 `move_base` and implemented Model Predictive Control (MPC) as our local planner. Here is the [formulation for our MPC](/assets/img_post/mlda-robo/Poster.pdf).
