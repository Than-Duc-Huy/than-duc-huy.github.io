## Description
This is a design project for the Body-Centric Design course at the School of Art Design Media (ADM) in NTU.

Our product includes an expressive lower leg prosthetics and an arm piece wearable to foster connection, involving the amputee's loved ones in their emotional journey throughout the recovery, rehabilitation process.
