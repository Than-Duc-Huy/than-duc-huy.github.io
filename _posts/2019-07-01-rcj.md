## Robocup Junior 2019 Sydney Onstage

The task was to build robots to make a performance. We managed to win the first place!
Our performance had 4 components:

- The highlight was the humanoid-shape (wheeled base) robot called Eve
- A laptop to record my motion and using [Openpose](https://github.com/CMU-Perceptual-Computing-Lab/openpose) for 2D pose detection to send command to Eve
- The guitar playing robot with many servos and a camera to decode a music script
- Repurposed Soccer holonomic robot flashing lights

### Post-competition development

After the competition, I extended the project by building an exo-skeleton to control Eve more directly instead of using the webcam. The exo-skeleton wrapped around the operator body and it had rotary encoders at the joints to map the joint angle on to Eve.
